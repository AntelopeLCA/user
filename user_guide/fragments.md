A life cycle model is made up of a collection of "flows" in the sense of LCA.  A flow can be literally anything, as long as it has some measurable "extent". Extensiveness is the defining quality of a flow in LCA, meaning that it has a magnitude that grows over time and can be measured.  Not all flows in LCA modeling are physical, but all of the emissions and impacts result only from activities with *physical* extent, be they quantities of plastic, a volume or mass transported over a distance, or an electric field conducted along a transmission line.
